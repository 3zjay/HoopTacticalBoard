📖 Hoop Tactical Pro: Quick Start Guide

🎨 1. The Environment & Setup

Use the Right-Hand Sidebar to select your court theme (Stealth Slate, NBA Cup, etc.) and toggle tactical overlays like the Spacing Heatmap and Deep 4-Point Arc.

⛹️ 2. Roster & Player Management

Drag players from the right sidebar onto the court. The defense (red nodes) features an Auto-Defense Alignment engine—just drag a defender near an offensive player and they will perfectly align between the ball handler and the hoop. Double-click any player to edit their initials.

✏️ 3. The Drawing Tools (Left Palette)

  • [V] Select / Move: Drag players and the ball.
  • [P] Pencil: Freehand draw.
  • [D] Dashed Cut: Draw passing lines.
  • [C] Curved Arrow: Draw smooth movement paths.
  • [S] Screen Pick: Draw a T-Bar to represent a screen.
  • [T] Coaching Note: Type text directly on the court.
  • [E] Eraser: Delete lines, players, or text.

⏱️ 4. The Timeline & FastBuild Engine

The timeline dock at the bottom lets you build animated playbooks. Draw paths for your players in Frame 1, then click the glowing FastBuild button. The engine will instantly teleport the players to the ends of their paths and create Frame 2 for you! Use the ▶ Play button to watch your sequence animate.

📖 5. The Pre-Loaded Playbook Library

Scroll down the right sidebar to access 33+ animated tactical sets. Selecting a play loads an interactive coaching HUD explaining the tactics and "WHAT-IF" scenarios frame-by-frame.
